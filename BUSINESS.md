# Audra — Business Overview

**One-liner:** One-click beat-to-video engine for music producers.

## Problem
Producers and beatmakers need professional music videos for YouTube/Shorts but editing software is slow and expensive, and hiring editors doesn't scale to a release cadence.

## Solution
Drop in a track + cover/visual, pick a preset (Ambient Drift, Trap Pulse, Cinematic Fade, Afro Glow, Hard Cut Energy) and Audra renders platform-ready videos via hardened FFmpeg pipelines — batch multi-format (16:9, 9:16, full + 30s teaser).

## Target market
Independent music producers, beatmakers, and content creators shipping regular releases.

## Competition
Rotor Videos, Specterr, CapCut templates. Edge: preset quality + one-click batch multi-format, priced for indie producers.

## Business model
Paid app / license via LemonSqueezy; tiers by render volume/quality.

## Key metrics
North star: **videos rendered**. KPIs: paying users, videos/user/month, render success rate.

## Moat
Curated preset aesthetic and rendering speed/reliability; a library that compounds with each new preset.

## Risks
CapCut/AI video commoditization. Mitigate by owning a producer-specific aesthetic and workflow.

## Status
Development. Stack: Python, FFmpeg, LemonSqueezy.
