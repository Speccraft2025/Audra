01–03 How It Works

One Upload. Every Format.

Audra was built for producers who'd rather make the next beat than fight a video editor.

🎧

1 — Drop Your Beat & Cover

Any common audio format, plus your cover art — a still image, or a looping video clip on Pro. Audra probes, repairs, and normalizes everything on ingest so renders never fail halfway through.

🎛️

2 — Pick a Preset & Platform

Choose a visual preset that matches the energy of the track, then select your targets: full-length 16:9 for YouTube, vertical 9:16 for TikTok and Shorts, and an auto-cut 30-second teaser.

🚀

3 — Render & Post

All variants render simultaneously from a single master upload, powered by low-level FFmpeg filter graphs — not a cloud queue. Your files stay on your machine, ready to post.
